02 / INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS

Research

I work on how robots sense, interpret, and act in the world. My research combines bio-inspired models, artificial intelligence, computer vision, and the engineering of complete robotic systems.

Research directions

01 / SENSING

Artificial tactile skin

Bio-inspired tactile sensing using Electrical Impedance Tomography. My doctoral work explored multifrequency measurements and AI for tactile imaging, including applications to autonomous vehicles.

02 / CONTROL

Bio-inspired robot control

Neural models for learning and replaying motor primitives in collaborative robots, combining sensory feedback and computer vision.

03 / PERCEPTION

Vision for movement

Recognising motor trajectories and detecting anomalous robot movement from visual data, including 3D-camera sensing.

04 / AUTONOMY

Autonomous robotics

Autonomous aerial and ground robots: visual recognition, person tracking, anomaly detection, LiDAR-based mapping, SLAM, localisation, and collaborative navigation.

05 / EMBODIMENT

Robotic locomotion

A tensegrity-based walking robot trained with reinforcement learning, exploring adaptive movement in a compliant body.
